<ins>Background Information:</ins> <br>
Homes and commercial spaces face a variety of security risks like unauthorized entry or even unexpected disturbances. To address these potential issues, the Home Owl project developed a low cost, responsive platform for real time indoor surveillance in environments such as warehouses, offices, and homes. The Home Owl robot combines autonomous navigation with disturbance detection using PIR and sound sensors and offers remote control activation as well. It autonomously traverses a room, pauses briefly to allow sensor stabilization for reliable motion and audio monitoring, and sets off an alarm upon detecting anomalies. The robot’s structure was designed with 3D printed components and laser cut acrylic, resulting in a modular architecture that supports easy future upgrades.
